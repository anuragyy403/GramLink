# 1. Problem & Vision

## Abstract

**GramLink** is an AI-powered village equipment and labor-sharing platform designed to connect farmers, equipment owners, and laborers through a single centralized digital platform.

Small and marginal farmers currently face significant difficulty accessing equipment and labor, relying heavily on word-of-mouth communication, phone calls, and scattered social media groups. This fragmented, informal approach makes it hard to find the right resources quickly or reliably.

GramLink solves this by enabling users to publish equipment availability, labor availability, equipment requirements, and labor requirements — all on one platform. An **AI-based recommendation engine** then analyzes these listings and suggests the most suitable matches based on **category, location, date, and text similarity**.

## Problem Statement

Small and marginal farmers often struggle to find affordable equipment and labor resources.

**Current methods relied upon:**
- Word-of-mouth communication
- Phone calls
- WhatsApp groups

**These methods result in:**
- Loss of time, money, and energy
- Delayed harvesting
- Underutilization of resources
- Reduced productivity

## Objectives

- Improve equipment utilization
- Reduce costs
- Reduce search time
- Connect equipment owners with farmers
- Connect laborers with employers
- Increase productivity

## Stakeholders

- Farmers
- Equipment owners
- Laborers
- Farmers needing equipment
- Farmers needing labor

## Target Users & Personas

| Target User | Role in the Platform |
|---|---|
| Farmers | General platform users — may post or seek either equipment or labor |
| Equipment owners | Publish equipment availability (tractors, harvesters, tillers, sprayers, seeders) |
| Laborers | Publish labor availability, including labor type and wage |
| Farmers needing equipment | Post equipment requirements — date, budget, duration |
| Farmers needing labor | Post labor requirements — number of laborers, work type, budget |
